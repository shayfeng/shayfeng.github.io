Selected Publications

Selected first-author work in tactile robot manipulation and robot learning.

IROS 2026First Author

TactileReflex: Noise-Statistics-Driven Vision-Tactile Reflex Control for Force-Sensitive Manipulation

A self-calibrating tactile reflex controller that uses sensor noise statistics instead of external force calibration, material models, or manually tuned thresholds.

ProblemLearned or teleoperated grasping can react too slowly to slip and excessive contact force.
MethodThree reflex channels for slip suppression, weight-adaptive release, and force protection at ~25 Hz.
Result90% success in dynamic pouring; irreversible deformation prevented in 5/5 soft-cup trials.
arXiv
CoRL 2026First Author

What is the Better Curriculum: Controller-Shaped Grasping Behavior for Contact Force-Sensitive Manipulation

Instead of adding tactile sensing to policy inference, high-rate tactile feedback shapes demonstrations during data collection so standard policies learn safer force-sensitive behavior.

ProblemNaively adding tactile inputs does not necessarily improve policy learning.
MethodKeep policy inputs and architecture fixed; change only the demonstration source using tactile closed-loop shaping.
ResultStable grasping: ACT 15%→95%, π0.5 5%→95%; tactile reflex re-engagement recovers disturbed executions.
